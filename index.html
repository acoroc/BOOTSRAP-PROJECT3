The Mythos of The E-Heads

Formed in 1989 at the University of the Philippines Diliman, the **Eraserheads** fundamentally reshaped the landscape of Philippine alternative rock. Blending raw garage-band energy, melodic structures reminiscent of classic pop, and clever, hyper-local storytelling, they gave a distinctive voice to the everyday experiences of Filipino youth throughout the 1990s.

The group—comprising **Ely Buendia**, **Buddy Zabala**, **Marcus Adoro**, and **Raimund Marasigan**—broke into the mainstream with their multi-platinum debut album *Ultraelectromagneticpop!*. Their masterful songwriting sparked an underground band explosion, pulling acoustic lyricism out of quiet venues and placing alternative sounds directly into mainstream radio stations across the archipelago.

"Their songs act like sonic checkpoints in modern Pinoy history. To hear 'Ang Huling El Bimbo' is to remember the exact texture of an entire generation's youth."

Beyond the chart success of timeless records like *Cutterpillow* and *Sticker Happy*, the Eraserheads earned prestigious international nods, including the iconic Billboard Asia Viewer's Choice Award at the 1997 MTV Video Music Awards. Decades after their initial separation, their catalog continues to influence contemporary music production, reinforcing their status as a cornerstone of modern Philippine art.

The Lineup

Often dubbed "The Beatles of the Philippines," meet the foundational architects of 90s OPM alternative rock.

Ely Buendia

Lead Vocals / Rhythm Guitar

The primary songwriter and iconic voice of the band. Meeting the group at UP Diliman, Ely's catchy melodies and witty, caustic storytelling fueled their multi-platinum breakthrough albums like Ultraelectromagneticpop! and Cutterpillow.

Buddy Zabala

Bass Guitar / Backing Vocals

The anchor of the band's rhythm section. Buddy's dynamic, driving basslines provided the tight musical backbone that defined the band's unique genre-spanning sound, blending elements of pop hooks, alternative rock, and punk.

Marcus Adoro

Lead Guitar / Backing Vocals

Known for his surf-rock inflections and distinct, raw tone. Marcus's iconic guitar riffs gave life to unmatched timeless anthems like "Ang Huling El Bimbo" and "Alapaap," securing his footprint in Pinoy rock history.

Raimund Marasigan

Drums / Keyboards / Backing

The experimental engine of the group. Raimund brought unparalleled energy from behind the drum kit while steering their later non-linear studio projects, pushing the band to integrate electronica and art-rock styles.
